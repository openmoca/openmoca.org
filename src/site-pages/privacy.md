**This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from other
sites.** It is a set of static pages. Nothing on it asks for your name, email or
any other personal information.

## What is processed to serve the site

openmoca.org is served by **Cloudflare**, which receives each request, including
your IP address and browser details, to deliver the pages and protect the site
from attacks. Cloudflare processes this for us under its own terms and
[privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). We do not use it
to identify you or to build profiles.

The site's search runs in your browser: what you type is not sent anywhere.

## When you contact us

If you email hello@openmoca.org, we use your message and address to reply, and
keep the conversation only as long as it is useful. Questions, issues and
discussions on GitHub are public and are covered by
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Try OpenMOCA

[try.openmoca.org](https://try.openmoca.org) is a separate site with its own
sign-in, and its own [privacy notice](https://try.openmoca.org/privacy).
