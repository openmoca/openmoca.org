## What is in it

Eight real works, each packaged from where it was published, and signed:

- **Learn Copilot Studio 2026**: a video series of 17 videos, in order. Every
  result opens the video at the moment. _MadeForCloud on YouTube, CC BY 3.0._
- **First Steps to Using Claude AI**: a recorded talk, opened at each chapter.
  _Eliot Prince on YouTube, CC BY 3.0._
- **Kubernetes documentation**: a software company's documentation, all 1,653
  pages. _The Kubernetes Authors, CC BY 4.0._
- **Airplane Flying Handbook**: a manual, with what each topic builds on.
  _FAA, public domain._
- **Data Protection Act 2018**: a law, section by section.
  _legislation.gov.uk, Open Government Licence._
- **HSE health and safety guidance**: safety rules, opened at the page of the
  PDF. _Health and Safety Executive, Open Government Licence._
- **GitLab handbook**: a company's policies and ways of working. _GitLab,
  Creative Commons with attribution._
- **Acme Support Knowledge Base**: an example help centre, in English and
  French. Internal pages stay out of public answers, and out-of-date policies
  are left out. _The OpenMOCA specification's example, Apache-2.0._

## What you can do there

- **Search, with no account.** Search by words, by meaning, or both. Your
  search stays on the demo's own computers; it is never sent to an AI company.
- **Open every result at its source.** The passage opens beside the results;
  a video opens at the moment.
- **Search within a part**, such as one chapter or one topic, and widen back
  out.
- **Check, don't trust.** See who signed each package, and that it has not
  been changed.
- **Ask it questions.** Sign in with your own AI key, or a demo key by
  invitation. Every answer names its sources, and each one opens the original.

The demo is built and run by PI & Mash on the open software; its own code is
not part of OpenMOCA.
