**Knowledge you can check.** Your organisation's knowledge travels: into
apps, into AI assistants, to partners and customers. OpenMOCA keeps with it
where it came from, which version it is, who signed it and on what terms, so
anyone who reads it can check.

## What it gives your knowledge

- **Provenance.** Every answer is a citation to a versioned page, with quotes
  checked against the original source, and the publisher and contributors
  named ([trust and provenance](/docs/trust-and-provenance/)).
- **Versions.** Pages and packages carry validity dates; a newer package can
  supersede an older one. Search leaves out what is no longer in force.
- **Signed packages.** One digest covers every file, so any change shows. A
  signature shows who signed, and signed reviews show who checked which page
  ([signing and review](/docs/guides/signing-and-review/)).
- **Terms that travel.** Your terms for AI use, export, ratings and rights go
  with every copy, and an app applies them when it asks
  ([the creator's terms](/docs/guides/creators-terms/)).
- **Any app.** Open Readers read a package the same way in any app, AI or
  not, on your servers or offline. You are not tied to one vendor.

## A package, in three layers

What each layer gives your knowledge: the content itself, its structure (the
order of a procedure, the parts of a handbook, what a lesson builds on), the
evidence behind it (the original sources, carried with it), and the trust
that covers all three.

## Who it suits

- **Publishers**, whose books, shows and series reach apps they don't run,
  and who want credits, sources and terms to go with them.
- **Learning providers**, whose courses keep their order and prerequisites,
  and whose answers open the lesson at the moment.
- **Companies with policies, procedures and guides**, who need answers from
  the version in force, internal pages kept internal, and a record of what
  each answer used.

More are in the [use cases](/docs/use-cases/).
