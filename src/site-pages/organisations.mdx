**Your knowledge travels:** into apps, into AI assistants, to partners and
customers. OpenMOCA keeps with it where it came from, which version it is, who
signed it and what you allow, so anyone who reads it can check.

## What it gives your knowledge

- **Where it came from.** Every answer links back to the exact page it came
  from, with quotes checked against the original, and the people who made it
  named ([trust and provenance](/docs/trust-and-provenance/)).
- **Which version.** Pages carry the dates they apply. A newer version can
  replace an older one, and search leaves out what no longer applies.
- **Who signed it.** A package is sealed, so any change shows. A signature
  shows who signed it, and a signed review shows who checked each page
  ([signing and review](/docs/guides/signing-and-review/)).
- **Your wishes travel.** What you allow for AI, copying, ratings and rights
  goes with every copy, and apps can follow it
  ([the creator's terms](/docs/guides/creators-terms/)).
- **Any app.** Free libraries read a package the same way in any app, with or
  without AI, on your own servers or offline. You are not tied to one
  supplier.

## A package, in three layers

What each layer gives your knowledge: the work itself, how it is organised
(the order of a procedure, the parts of a handbook, what a lesson builds on),
the original sources behind it, and the seal and signatures that cover all
three.

## Who it suits

- **Publishers**, whose books, shows and series reach apps they don't run,
  and who want credits, sources and wishes to go with them.
- **Learning providers**, whose courses keep their order and what comes
  first, and whose answers open the lesson at the right moment.
- **Companies with policies, procedures and guides**, who need answers from
  the version in force, internal pages kept internal, and a record of what
  each answer used.

More are in the [use cases](/docs/use-cases/).
