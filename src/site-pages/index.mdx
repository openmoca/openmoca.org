## Work travels. The people behind it get left behind.

Every day, podcasts, books, courses and guides are copied, quoted, summarised
and searched — by websites, by apps, and more and more by AI. Somewhere along
the way, the important things fall off: who made it, where it came from, and
how they want it used.

Links used to send you to the maker. Now apps answer for them. You get the
answer, but not the person behind it. Creators lose the credit; you lose the
chance to check.

## OpenMOCA keeps them together.

OpenMOCA is a free, open way to package any digital work so its context goes
wherever it goes: the people who made it, the sources it came from, and the
wishes of its creator. Any app can read it — a podcast player, an e-reader, a
learning app, or an AI assistant.

## How it works

1. **Package it.** The creator wraps their work with its credits, sources and
   wishes, and seals it.
2. **Use it anywhere.** Any app that reads OpenMOCA can open it, search it,
   quote from it with a link back to the original, and use it with or without
   AI, following the creator's wishes.
3. **Check it.** Anyone can see who made it, open the original, and see what
   the creator asked. Apps can [promise publicly](/commitment/) to honour it.
