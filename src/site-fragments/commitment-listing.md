## Apps that have made the Commitment

PI & Mash maintains OpenMOCA, and is listed here under the same rules as
anyone else.

### Try OpenMOCA, by PI & Mash

- **The app:** [Try OpenMOCA](https://try.openmoca.org), the OpenMOCA demo.
- **The company:** PI & Mash Solutions Ltd.
- **Version:** 0.7.0.
- **AI models, and where your work goes:** searches use two open models,
  bge-small-en-v1.5 and nomic-embed-text-v1.5, on the demo's own server, and
  are never sent to an AI provider. Answers come from an AI model provided by
  Anthropic, for people with an invitation or approved access.
- **Set-ups covered:** search, and answers through the demo's own access to
  Anthropic. Not covered: answers when you use your own API key with another
  provider.
- **Contact for challenges:**
  [hello@openmoca.org](mailto:hello@openmoca.org).
- **PI & Mash's Commitment statement:** signed record to follow.

## How a company makes the Commitment

1. **Check your app** against the list above, "For companies: what you commit
   to", for the version people will use.
2. **Write a signed statement** that names your company, the app, the versions
   it covers, the date, the AI models and destinations the app uses, the
   set-ups it covers, and a contact for challenges. It is signed with the same
   format packages use.
3. **Ask for it to be listed**, at
   [hello@openmoca.org](mailto:hello@openmoca.org). Once it is listed, the app
   may show the Commitment mark.

A new version of the app needs a new statement. A listing that is withdrawn,
or shown to be untrue, is removed. Software that reads OpenMOCA is listed
separately, as [OpenMOCA Conformant](/directory/conformant/).
